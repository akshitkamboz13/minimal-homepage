Labs & Experimental Features Warning

Labs

Features tagged with Labs (such as Freeform Drag & Drop Screen Layout Rearrangement) are active testing features in continuous development.

  • Stability: Labs features may undergo layout changes, variations, or experimental updates.
  • Testing Purpose: Designed for early feedback, customization testing, and power-user experimentation.
  • Recovery: If you experience unexpected layout displacement, click Restore Default Layout under Windows settings anytime.

Privacy Policy & Data Transparency

Home by si4k is built with strict privacy-first principles. Your data belongs exclusively to you.

  • 100% Client-Side Execution: No tracking scripts, no telemetry analytics, and zero external data collection.
  • Local Storage Only: Your notes, settings, background colors, and custom element coordinates remain strictly in your browser (localStorage & chrome.storage.local).
  • Bookmarks & Permissions: Chrome permissions are used exclusively to display your local browser bookmarks bar. No bookmark data ever leaves your device.
